# Consent to the Collection and Processing of Biometric Data
*(Attendance System — template, review with your Data Protection Officer)*

I, ______________________________ (Employee ID: __________), understand that:

1. **What is collected:** The company's attendance system creates a numerical template of my face from a camera scan. **No photograph of me is stored.**
2. **Purpose:** The template is used **only** to record my time in and time out.
3. **Storage and access:** The template and my attendance records are stored in a secured database. Only authorized HR/admin personnel can access them.
4. **Retention:** My face template is deleted when I leave the company or withdraw this consent. Attendance records are kept as required by labor laws.
5. **My rights:** Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), I may access, correct, or ask for deletion of my data, and I may withdraw my consent at any time. If I withdraw, I will use an alternative attendance method (e.g., manual logbook verified by my supervisor).
